From question to handoff

Start small. Make the scope explicit.

A useful engagement begins with a defined question and agreed acceptance criteria, not a promise that a prototype fits every problem.

  1. Share a non-confidential overview of your objective and desired outcome.
  2. Agree in writing on the scope, fees, timing, access, and deliverables.
  3. Review the resulting work against the agreed criteria and documented limitations.
